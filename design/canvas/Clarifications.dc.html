Regulator clarifications

CL-014ANSWERED · NOT YET APPLIED

Does an implementation sample from the previous cycle satisfy integration requirements if the systems are unchanged?

Raised by Faisal K. (IT Operations) · submitted to DGA 11 Oct via portal inquiry · response 24 Oct
AUTHORITY RESPONSE · VERBATIM
يجب أن يكون النموذج التطبيقي من الدورة الحالية، ولا يُقبل نموذج الدورة السابقة حتى في حال عدم تغيّر الأنظمة.
Source: DGA portal response #R-5521 · attachment kept
FINAL INTERPRETATION · APPROVED
Implementation samples must be dated within the current cycle. Prior-cycle samples are not acceptable even for unchanged systems.
